How it works

Your agent runs the interview. You just answer.

Say “interview me about this feature” or “grill me on the migration”. The skill does the rest through the kim CLI.

Your coding agent
Asks the questionsChoices, yes/no, numbers or free text, a few at a time
Recommends answersIts pick pre-selected, with a line on why
Keeps the planRewritten after every batch of answers
Shows its workDiagrams and mockups beside the questions
You answer in the browserOr ask back in the chat
It keeps goingAnswers reach it in seconds, even tomorrow
End-to-end encrypted

We can’t read your interviews.

Questions, answers, chat, plans and mockups are encrypted on your devices and on your agent’s machine before they reach us. Our servers only ever hold ciphertext, so a leak, a subpoena or a curious engineer gets nothing readable.

Your keys, your devices

A passkey unlocks your interviews on a new device, and kim login hands your CLI its key straight from the browser. Someone you assign an interview to gets it wrapped to their own key.

What we can still see

Who an interview is with, when things happen, how many questions are answered, how big things are, and which repo it’s for. Not what any of it says.

Keep your recovery code

Lose your passkey and your recovery code, and your encrypted interviews are gone for good: we hold no copy of your key, so we can’t get them back either.

The web app is served by us, so it’s only as trustworthy as the code we ship, which you can read on GitHub. Interviews from before encryption, or from an account that hasn’t turned it on, aren’t encrypted.
