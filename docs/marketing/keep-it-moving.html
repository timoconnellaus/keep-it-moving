Plan it properly

Design it in the interview. Ship it in the PR.

Work through the data model, architecture and flows with your agent. Start from what's already in your repo, and end with updated files your reviewers can read.

1

Starts from your repo

Your agent pulls the data model or API it needs from .kim/ in your repo. Nothing else clutters the screen.

2

Every change, highlighted

New tables in green, changed fields in amber, compared with main. Pin a comment on any box or arrow.

3

Lands in your PR

When the interview ends, the designs your agent kept and the plan are written back, with a picture of each for review.

4

Six kinds of design

Data models, architecture, data flow, sequences, state machines and API contracts, in DBML, D2, Mermaid and OpenAPI.

How it works

Your agent runs the interview. You just answer.

Say “interview me about this feature” or “grill me on the migration”. The skill does the rest through the kim CLI.

Your coding agent
Asks the questionsChoices, yes/no, numbers or free text, a few at a time
Recommends answersIts pick pre-selected, with a line on why
Keeps the planRewritten after every batch of answers
Shows its workDiagrams and mockups beside the questions
You answer in the browserOr ask back in the chat
It keeps goingAnswers reach it in seconds, even tomorrow
Writes it backDesigns and the plan land in .kim/ for your PR
